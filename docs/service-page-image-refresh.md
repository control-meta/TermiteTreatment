## Prompt set

All prompts specified a wide 3:2 website hero, realistic Indian service setting, natural light, editorial commercial photography, no text, logo or watermark, and a distinct scene.

- **Residential:** A candid Indian pest-control specialist in a clean dark green work uniform carefully inspecting the wooden skirting and cabinet edge in a warm, lived-in Indian apartment; natural window light, realistic timber grain and subtle signs of termite damage, no visible chemicals, reassuring domestic atmosphere.
- **Commercial:** A building-care professional in a deep green uniform checking a discreet wooden reception counter in a contemporary Indian office lobby, glass walls and desks softly visible, tablet in hand, calm professional environment, realistic light and material textures.
- **Pre-construction:** Ground-level view at an active Indian building site before concrete is poured: a trained technician in green PPE applying soil treatment along a foundation trench with a professional hose and measured spray, builders and steel reinforcement softly in the distance, accurate construction details, morning light.
- **Post-construction:** Close candid view of a gloved technician using a small precision injection tool at a discreet drilled point along the skirting of a finished Indian home, clean finished wall and wood floor, subtle mud tube evidence nearby, controlled professional treatment, warm natural light.
- **Annual maintenance:** A pest-care specialist in a green uniform conducting a scheduled preventive inspection in a landscaped apartment building corridor, holding a tablet checklist and examining a wooden door frame, bright Indian daylight, neat property, sense of continuity and care.
